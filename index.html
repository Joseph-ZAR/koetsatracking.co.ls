THE CROSS-BORDER REALITY

Vehicle crime can cross a border in minutes. Your response needs to be ready.

Lesotho and South Africa share close travel, family and business links. That connection makes visibility, timely information and cooperation important when a vehicle goes missing.

A concern in Lesotho

Vehicle theft is a real security issue.

In September 2025, the Lesotho News Agency reported that vehicle theft remained a challenge in the country and covered a partnership between a tracking provider and the Lesotho Mounted Police Service to strengthen tracing and recovery efforts.

Read the Lesotho News Agency report ↗
A documented cross-border case

Stolen vehicles have been found across the border.

During Operation Siyaqoqa in 2024, South African police reported recovering several vehicles in Mafeteng, Lesotho, that had been stolen in Gauteng — including a Ford Ranger, Toyota Fortuner, BMW and Toyota Quantum. The case shows why cross-border vehicle crime requires information-sharing and coordinated action.

Read the Operation Siyaqoqa report ↗

These examples describe reported cases and the wider cross-border risk. They are not a claim that every stolen vehicle follows the same route or that a tracking device guarantees recovery.
