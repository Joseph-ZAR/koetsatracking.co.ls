THE VALUE OF A MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTION

More than a tracking device. A team ready to respond.

With an active monthly subscription, Koetsa provides an ongoing service relationship. Our Operations Centre receives SOS alerts, contacts the client, assesses the threat and coordinates the appropriate response.

KOETSA OPERATIONS CENTRE

Tracking and response managed by our team

Security & Control, Fleet & Driver Intelligence and Premium Recovery & Response include continuous vehicle monitoring by Koetsa. Track & Trace is a customer-requested location service: call Koetsa when you need a location check. All four active monthly subscriptions include the SOS emergency-response protocol.

Full upfront package purchases include the selected package's standard equipment and features, but do not include continuous monitoring by our office or Koetsa-coordinated panic-button emergency response.

SOS PANIC-BUTTON PROTOCOL

Press the button. We take it from there.

  1. Alert receivedThe panic-button notification reaches our Operations Centre.
  2. Client contactedOur team calls you to assess the threat and understand the situation.
  3. Threat assessedWe determine the appropriate escalation based on your location and circumstances.
  4. Response teams coordinatedWhere appropriate, we alert SAPS, the Lesotho Defence Force (LDF), the Lesotho Mounted Police Service (LMPS), and/or our private armed recovery partners.
  5. Follow-up and coordinationWe maintain communication as the response develops.

No additional Koetsa emergency-response activation fee for clients with an active monthly subscription.

Illustrative response scenario. Actual response depends on the threat, location, jurisdiction and availability of the relevant authorities and response partners.

Important: Koetsa coordinates and initiates the agreed response protocol. Attendance by external authorities or response partners cannot be guaranteed and remains subject to their jurisdiction, operational procedures and availability.

SERVICE STATUS & PAYMENT TERMS

Keep your protection active.

The monthly subscription keeps your tracking service and emergency-response eligibility active.

More than two months in arrears

If a monthly account remains unpaid for more than two months, Koetsa may deactivate the tracking device and suspend the associated subscription services. While the account is suspended, SOS notifications will not activate Koetsa's emergency-response protocol. Service and emergency-response eligibility are restored only after the account is brought fully up to date and Koetsa confirms reactivation.
