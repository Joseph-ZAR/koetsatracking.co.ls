TRACKING HARDWARE & SOLUTIONS

Technology matched to what you protect.

Explore the tracking technology behind our solutions. We describe the products by what they do—not by factory model numbers. Final device selection depends on network compatibility, installation requirements and the service agreed with Koetsa.

ESSENTIAL VEHICLE TRACKER · WIRED⌖

Essential Vehicle Tracker

A compact wired tracker for motorcycles, cars, vans, buses and trucks. The product information describes a waterproof IP65 unit with a wide 9–90V input range.

  • GPS / BeiDou positioning
  • Live location and route history
  • Electronic geofencing
  • Offline, over-speed and vibration alerts
  • Power-disconnect / low-power alerts
  • Remote engine cut-off where compatible
  • Location checks by app, web or SMS
  • ACC / ignition detection on Pro model
COVERT SECURITY TRACKER · CONCEALED▣

Covert Security Tracker

A relay-style wired unit designed to be concealed in compatible vehicles. Available product descriptions include 2G and 4G versions for vehicle and scooter tracking.

  • Real-time app / web tracking
  • ACC and ignition status
  • Historical route playback
  • Geofence and over-speed alerts
  • Vibration and low-power alerts
  • Wire-cut / power-disconnect alerts
  • Remote cut-off and restore functions
  • Hidden installation format
FLEET INTELLIGENCE TRACKER · SOS-READY✚

Fleet Intelligence & SOS Tracker

A vehicle tracking unit described for fleet management, with an SOS button, voice-monitoring capability and remote engine-control functions.

  • Live tracking by app and PC
  • SOS emergency calling
  • Voice monitoring
  • Remote fuel / electrical cut-off and restore
  • Over-speed and vibration alerts
  • Low-battery and power cut alerts
  • Movement and ignition on/off alerts
  • Geofences and historical routes
ASSET & CARGO GUARDIAN · WIRELESS⬡

Asset & Cargo Guardian

A battery-powered magnetic tracker for movable assets and equipment. The 4G product description lists a 6,800mAh battery and long standby potential, depending on usage and network conditions.

  • Strong magnetic attachment
  • Real-time location and route history
  • Geofence and movement alerts
  • Removal / tamper alert
  • Vibration and displacement alerts
  • Voice-monitoring capability
  • Fall-off alert
  • Suitable for cargo, containers and boats

Choosing a package: Koetsa service packages are named Track & Trace, Security & Control, Fleet & Driver Intelligence, and Premium Recovery & Response. These describe levels of service, not manufacturer device models. We will match the appropriate hardware to your vehicle, asset and selected package. Package prices remain on the Packages & Pricing page.

Important: Product specifications are manufacturer descriptions, not a promise that every feature is included in every Koetsa package. Network generation, local coverage, device variant, platform configuration, installation and any remote cut-off or SOS function must be confirmed before supply or activation. Remote immobilisation must only be used when the vehicle is safely stationary and the installation is approved.
