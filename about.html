About Koetsa

Built from a practical challenge into a company focused on protection, reliability and service.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

A Lesotho-based telematics company.

Koetsa Tracking Systems (Pty) Ltd is a dynamic telematics company headquartered in Lesotho, specializing in state-of-the-art tracking solutions.

Our name, “Koetsa”, embodies our promise of security and protection. We aim to deliver peace of mind through tracking technology, real-time monitoring and responsive support.

THE GENESIS OF KOETSA

From a personal challenge to an entrepreneurial journey.

The company was born from the entrepreneurial journey of founder Mr. Patrick Rangoajane. While a student at Limkokwing Creative University of Technology, he identified a gap in the local market for vehicle owners’ tracking requirements. He developed that opportunity into a sustainable business after graduation. His “go-getter” attitude is now embedded in Koetsa’s culture.

A WORD FROM THE CEO

Founder’s message

“What began as a pragmatic solution to a personal challenge has blossomed into a company with a profound mission: to safeguard what matters most to people — their vehicles, their assets, and most importantly, their families.”

Koetsa’s ambition is to become Lesotho’s undisputed leader in premium tracking systems by setting a high standard of excellence, reliability and trust. Our strategic initiatives and partnerships are intended to extend protection beyond Lesotho’s borders.

Mr. Patrick Rangoajane
Founder & Chief Executive Officer
