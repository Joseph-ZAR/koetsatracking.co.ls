WHO WE ARE

Security is more than technology. It is peace of mind.

Koetsa Tracking Systems (Pty) Ltd is a Lesotho-based telematics company headquartered in Upper Thamae, Maseru. We provide tracking solutions designed to help individuals, families and organisations stay informed about their vehicles, people and valuable assets.

Our work brings together GPS tracking, fleet visibility, smart alerts and responsive customer support. From everyday journeys to business operations and cross-border travel, we believe that useful information and dependable service can make a meaningful difference.

Our name, Koetsa, reflects our commitment to security and protection. We aim to build lasting relationships through reliability, integrity and a client-centred approach.

THE GENESIS OF KOETSA

A local idea with a bigger purpose.

Koetsa grew from recognising a practical need among vehicle owners in Lesotho.

Founded by Mr. Patrick Rangoajane, Koetsa began during his student years at Limkokwing University of Creative Technology. After identifying a gap in the local market for vehicle-tracking services, he began installing tracking devices for people within his network. What started as a practical opportunity developed into a business with a wider ambition: making dependable tracking and protection solutions more accessible to individuals and organisations in Lesotho.

That entrepreneurial beginning continues to shape the company’s approach — understand the real challenge, use technology purposefully, and put the customer at the centre of the solution.
