Extending tracking visibility for journeys across the region.

Regional connectivity

The company profile states that, once the partnership agreement is signed, Koetsa devices will seamlessly connect to partner networks across the SADC region to support uninterrupted tracking.

Strategic partnership for cross-border vehicle recovery

Koetsa describes an intended exclusive partnership with a South African security firm. Under the proposed arrangement, if a tracked vehicle is stolen and taken into a neighbouring country, the partner would deploy a specialized team to the vehicle’s location, using regional expertise and law-enforcement relationships.

The profile also states that discussions are at an advanced stage to establish Koetsa’s own in-house recovery team to further enhance response capabilities.

Availability: The profile makes these arrangements conditional on agreements and ongoing development. Please contact Koetsa to confirm current partner coverage and recovery arrangements before relying on this service.
